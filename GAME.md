## Rules of engagement

> Six lines on duct tape above the crash cart.

1. Mechanism — know network, identity, and state before you change them.
2. Least privilege — no long-lived keys in CI; use federation.
3. GitOps — desired state lives in git, not on a laptop.
4. Supply chain — scan and harden in every build.
5. Observability — uptime, cost, and recovery time are the score.
6. Outcomes — blast radius beats tool fashion.
